Bananagrams - standard rules (physical game)
============================================

Object
------
Be the first player to use all your letters in a valid, connected crossword-style
grid and call "BANANAS!" when the Bunch is nearly empty.


Setup
-----
1. Place all 144 letter tiles face down in the center. This is the BUNCH.
2. Each player draws starting tiles from the BUNCH (keep them face down):
     2-4 players: 21 tiles each
     5-6 players: 15 tiles each
     7-8 players: 11 tiles each
3. Leave the rest of the tiles in the BUNCH.


Start
-----
Any player calls "SPLIT!" Everyone flips their tiles face up at the same time.


Building your grid
------------------
- Each player builds their own grid. There are no turns; everyone plays at once.
- Words must intersect like a crossword. The whole grid must stay connected.
- Words read left-to-right (horizontal) or top-to-bottom (vertical). No diagonals.
- Minimum word length: 2 letters (house rules sometimes use 3+).
- You may rearrange your entire grid at any time.
- Valid words only: standard dictionary English. No proper nouns, abbreviations,
  or foreign words unless your group agrees otherwise.
- For our digital version, use the compiled tournament lexicon (NWL/CSW build).


PEEL
----
When you place your last face-up letter into your grid, call "PEEL!"
Every player (including you) immediately draws one tile from the BUNCH.


DUMP
----
At any time you may call "DUMP!":
  - Return one of your letters face down to the BUNCH.
  - Draw three new tiles from the BUNCH.
Other players are not affected. You may DUMP as often as you like.


Winning
-------
When the BUNCH has fewer tiles than the number of players still in the hand,
the first player to use ALL their letters in a connected grid calls "BANANAS!"


Challenge (Rotten Banana)
-------------------------
After "BANANAS!", other players may inspect the winning grid.
If any word is misspelled or otherwise unacceptable:
  - They call "ROTTEN BANANA!"
  - That player is out for this hand.
  - Their tiles go back face down into the BUNCH.
  - Remaining players continue until someone wins with a valid grid.


Common variants (not standard)
------------------------------
- Smoothie: split all tiles equally; no BUNCH; no PEEL.
- Banana Solitaire: one player, 21 tiles, PEEL when needed; beat your time.
- Restaurant: 21 tiles each, DUMP allowed, no PEEL.

Sources: Official Bananagrams rules sheet (Bananagrams, Inc., FoxMind).
