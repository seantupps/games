Quoridor (two-player)
=====================

Abstract strategy game on a 9×9 grid of squares. Each player has one pawn and
ten walls. First to reach the opposite side wins.

Sources: Gigamic Quoridor rules; reference implementation
https://github.com/gorisanson/quoridor-ai (vendored under ref/quoridor-ai/).

Build & play
------------
  Hub game (SP human vs AI, like line/piles):
    npm run serve
    open hub → Quoridor

  SP audit:
    npm run sp:quoridor

  Terminal CLI (Python / Numba):
    python build/quoridor/play/play.py
    quoridor [--seed S | --2p | --ai greedy|random]



Board and coordinates
---------------------
Squares: files a–i (left→right), ranks 1–9 (bottom→top).

  • Light (cyan O) starts at e1, goal = rank 9, moves first
  • Dark  (yellow O) starts at e9, goal = rank 1

Wall notation uses the center tick the wall sits on (matches board labels):

  H c5   horizontal wall on the rank-5 line, covering files c–d
         (between ranks 6 and 5)

  V e5   vertical wall on the rank-5 tick between e|f
         (covers ranks 6 and 5 — bar centered on the "5" label)


Setup
-----
1. Place light pawn at e1, dark pawn at e9.
2. Each player gets 10 walls.
3. Light moves first.


Play
----
On your turn, do exactly one of:

  A. Move your pawn one step orthogonally (N/S/E/W) into an empty, adjacent
     square not blocked by a wall.

  B. Jump the opponent’s pawn if they sit on an orthogonally adjacent square
     and the way forward is open: land in the square directly beyond them.
     If that square is blocked (wall or board edge), you may jump diagonally
     to either side of them instead (only when the straight jump is blocked).

  C. Place a wall (if you have any left). A wall covers two consecutive
     fence segments. Walls may not overlap or cross an existing wall, and
     after placement both pawns must still have some path to their goal
     line.


Winning
-------
Reach any square on your goal rank. Light wins on rank 9; dark wins on rank 1.


Move notation (terminal)
------------------------
  e2       move pawn to e2
  wasd     move N/W/S/E (straight jump if that direction is a legal jump)
  H c5     horizontal wall centered on c / rank-5 ticks
  V e5     vertical wall centered on e|f / rank-5 tick
  u        undo last turn (P1 + P2) in vs-AI mode
  help     show commands
  quit     exit
